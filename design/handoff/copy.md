## Section label: WHAT IT IS

HackAotearoa is a free co-working day for startup founders, indie hackers, designers and makers building their own thing. Every second Friday in Auckland: one full day, working alongside other people doing the same. No program, no curriculum, no pitching.

On any given Friday there's someone launching a website, someone wiring up an AI agent, someone chasing first customers, someone rewriting a pitch. Different projects, same fight.

- **Momentum.** A full day carved out to ship, next to people doing the same.
- **Getting unstuck.** Someone in the room has hit your problem before: pricing, landing pages, that 2pm bug.
- **People who get it.** Building your own thing is lonely. This is the fix.

80+ builders through the door since March.

## Section label: QUESTIONS

**Is this a hackathon?**
No. No shared challenge, no teams, no prize, nothing to prepare. Bring whatever you're already building, or just an idea and a laptop.

**Is this an incubator or accelerator?**
Also no. No equity, no program, no application, no demo day. An incubator takes a slice of your company. This takes a Friday.

**Do I need a startup?**
No. Founders, indie hackers, freelancers, side-project builders. If you're building your own thing, you're in.

**I'm not a developer. Is this for me?**
Yes. Designers, marketers, product people and writers are regulars. "Building" means making anything real: a landing page, a pitch, a first customer conversation.

**What's the catch?**
There isn't one. It's free, the venue is sponsored, and nobody is selling you anything.
